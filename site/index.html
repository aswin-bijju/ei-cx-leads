How it works

It runs on your AI's own web search. No account, no key, no data bought from anyone.

01

Tell it what you sell

And which way it goes. It fills in the rest of the brief from your website and shows it back before it searches.

02

It finds the signals

Capability centres, new Indian entities, senior hires, tenders, sourcing moves and CBAM exposure, searched in English and German.

03

It checks each one

Announced is kept apart from done, reported from confirmed. Anything it cannot source is dropped, and it tells you why.

04

You get ten that matter

Up to ten ranked accounts a week, with both sides of the committee and an act-by date. In Claude Code, also as a CSV.

What a lead looks like

Every lead carries its evidence. You can open the source, see how old it is, and see what it does not prove yet.

  • Announced is not done. An MoU is not a contract. Status and verification are two separate fields, never merged.
  • Two regions, one committee. The roles at the European HQ and in India, plus the works council step for German, Austrian and Dutch accounts.
  • Timing on both calendars. India's April to March fiscal year, Diwali, European holidays and works-council lead times.
  • Nothing invented. People are named only when a public source names them in that role. No guessed emails or phone numbers.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs on your AI's own web search, so there's no account, no key and nothing to top up. We earn from the Verified Signal Pack and the EI Cx Pilot. The skill is free to install and use for your own or your organisation's work. All rights are reserved, so please don't copy, change or redistribute it.

Where do the signals come from?

Public sources only: company sites and career pages, company registries, stock exchanges, government releases, the EU's tender journal (TED) and the business press in English and German. Every lead links to its source.

Does it send messages for me?

No. It drafts first messages when you ask, and you send them from your own account.

Does it give me emails and phone numbers?

No. It gives the roles to reach, and names people only when a public source names them in that role. We don't guess contact details.

What about GDPR and German email rules?

It flags them when they apply. In Germany, marketing emails need the recipient's prior consent under § 7 UWG, between businesses too, so it suggests a LinkedIn note, an introduction or a call instead. It is not legal advice.

What is EI Cx?

AIA's decision engine for B2B teams selling between Europe and India. Instead of dashboards, it gives a sales leader a short ranked list of decisions, each with its evidence. EI Cx Leads is its lead-finding part.
