# Where to look

Public sources only. If a source needs a login, is behind a paywall or blocks automated reading, use what web search shows about it, mark the signal `reported`, and look for a second source.

## Primary records (these make a signal `confirmed`)

| Source | Region | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The company's own newsroom, career site and annual report | Both | Openings, hires, programmes, live job counts |
| Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), company master data | India | New Indian subsidiaries, registered office, date of incorporation |
| BSE and NSE corporate announcements | India | Deals, acquisitions and joint ventures of listed Indian companies |
| Press Information Bureau (pib.gov.in) | India | Government outcomes, MoUs and visits, such as the list of outcomes of the German Chancellor's visit, 12 and 13 January 2026 |
| State IT and industry departments (Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu) | India | Capability-centre and plant announcements, often with the investment and job numbers |
| Unternehmensregister and Handelsregister | Germany | Company facts, subsidiaries, management changes |
| TED, Tenders Electronic Daily (ted.europa.eu) | EU | Public tenders with CPV codes and deadlines |
| European Commission trade and taxation pages | EU | Current status of the EU-India FTA, CBAM phases and thresholds |
| Official journals and regulators (EUR-Lex, national regulators) | EU | What a rule requires and from when |

## Secondary sources (`reported` until a second source agrees)

| Source | Region | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Economic Times, Mint, Business Standard, Moneycontrol, The Hindu BusinessLine | India | Capability centres, hires, deals, sector news |
| Handelsblatt, FAZ, WirtschaftsWoche, Automobilwoche, VDI nachrichten | Germany | DACH companies' India plans, often first in German |
| Het Financieele Dagblad, Les Echos, Financial Times | EU | Benelux, French and pan-European company news |
| NASSCOM and Zinnov capability-centre reports | India | Lists and counts of capability centres by city and sector |
| Indo-German Chamber of Commerce (AHK India) and Germany Trade & Invest (GTAI) | Corridor | Members' India projects, events, market notes |
| Trade press for the buyer's sector | Both | Plant openings, sourcing moves, tenders |
| Public profiles of named people | Both | Confirming that a person holds a role now. Never as the only source for a company signal |

## Checks before you use a source

- The date on the page is the date of the event or the article, not the date you read it.
- The company name, country and domain match the account. Many corridor companies share names.
- An article that only repeats a company's press release is the same source, not a second one.
- Numbers (investment, seats, jobs) come with whoever stated them: "the company says 300 seats", not "300 seats".
