# Signal catalogue

A signal is an event that makes it likely an account will buy in the next one to two quarters. Each entry gives its strength, its maximum age (older than this means drop it), how to confirm it, and search patterns to start from. Replace the words in angle brackets with terms from the brief. Search in the language the news breaks in first.

Strength feeds the "Signal strength" factor in the ranking: strong = 3, medium = 2, weak = 1.

## EU to India

The seller is European. The buyers are European companies building or growing operations in India, such as capability centres (GCCs), delivery teams, plants or sales entities.

### E1. Capability centre or delivery centre announced, opened or expanding
- **Why it means buying now:** a new or growing site needs tools, services, hiring, facilities and compliance from its first months.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 9 months for an announcement, 12 months for an opening.
- **Confirm with:** the company's own release, a state government release (Karnataka, Telangana, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu), an office address, or live site-leader or bulk job posts on the company's career site.
- **Search:**
  - `"global capability centre" OR "GCC" India <industry> <HQ country> 2026`
  - `<industry> company opens centre Pune OR Bengaluru OR Hyderabad OR Chennai`
  - German: `Entwicklungszentrum Indien eröffnet`, `"Standort in Indien" Pune OR Bangalore`, `Technologiezentrum Indien <Branche>`

### E2. Indian subsidiary incorporated
- **Why:** a legal entity comes before hiring, leases and local buying.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 6 months.
- **Confirm with:** the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) company master data, or a report in the Indian business press that names the new entity.
- **Search:** `"<company>" India "private limited" incorporated`, `<HQ country> company sets up Indian subsidiary <industry> 2026`

### E3. Senior India hire
- **Why:** a new leader in a new role usually buys within the first six months.
- **Roles:** GCC head, site leader, managing director India, country manager India, head of India operations.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 4 months.
- **Confirm with:** the company's announcement, a trade-press article, or the person's public profile showing the new role.
- **Search:** `appointed "head of" GCC India <industry>`, `"managing director India" appointed <HQ country> company`, German: `Geschäftsführer Indien ernannt`

### E4. Bulk hiring in an Indian city for a European employer
- **Why:** headcount growth pulls tooling, services and facilities with it.
- **Strength:** medium (strong if 50+ open roles). **Maximum age:** 60 days.
- **Confirm with:** live job posts on the company's own career site. Count them, and note the city and functions.
- **Search:** `"<company>" careers Pune OR Bengaluru OR Hyderabad`, `<company> hiring India engineering centre`

### E5. Leadership change at the European HQ
- **Why:** new leaders review vendors, footprints and the India programme.
- **Roles:** CIO, CTO, COO, CFO, head of global delivery, head of shared services.
- **Strength:** medium. **Maximum age:** 4 months.
- **Confirm with:** the company's release or website.
- **Search:** `"<company>" appoints CIO OR COO 2026`, German: `"<Firma>" neuer CIO OR COO ernannt`

### E6. Acquisition of, or joint venture with, an Indian company
- **Why:** integration needs systems, processes, compliance and people.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 6 months.
- **Confirm with:** releases from both companies, or BSE or NSE filings when the Indian side is listed.
- **Search:** `<HQ country> company acquires Indian <industry> firm 2026`, `joint venture India <industry> <HQ country> 2026`

### E7. A regulatory deadline that hits the account
- **Rules:** CSRD reporting, CSDDD, LkSG, EUDR, DORA, CBAM.
- **Why:** the account needs data, audits or supplier information from India by a date.
- **Strength:** medium (strong when the offer is the compliance answer). **Maximum age:** until the deadline passes.
- **Confirm with:** an official EU or national source for the rule and its current dates (dates and thresholds have changed, so always check), plus the company's own report or statement showing that it is exposed.
- **Search:** `"<company>" CSRD report India suppliers`, `<industry> CBAM India suppliers emissions data`

### E8. A delegation, MoU or government outcome that names the company
- **Example:** the outcomes of the German Chancellor's visit to India on 12 and 13 January 2026, listed by India's Press Information Bureau (PIB).
- **Why:** shows intent at the top. A budget may follow, or may not.
- **Strength:** weak. **Maximum age:** 12 months.
- **Confirm with:** the PIB, ministry or chamber release. Treat it as `announced` until something on the ground confirms it.
- **Search:** `site:pib.gov.in <company> OR <industry> Germany India`, `Indo-German MoU <industry> 2026`

## India to EU

The seller is Indian, for example in IT, analytics, engineering services or exports. The buyers are European companies such as banks, OEMs and retailers that buy from or partner with Indian companies.

### I1. A European company moves sourcing to India
- **Examples:** a "China plus one" move, a new sourcing office in India, a supplier programme for India.
- **Why:** the shift creates supplier, quality, logistics and compliance needs.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 9 months.
- **Confirm with:** the company's release, an interview with an executive, or the opening of a sourcing office.
- **Search:** `<industry> "sourcing office" India <HQ country> 2026`, `"China plus one" India <industry> European`, German: `Einkaufsbüro Indien`, `Beschaffung Indien ausbauen`

### I2. Vendor consolidation, outsourcing or a transformation programme
- **Why:** a formal buying process with a time window.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** 6 months.
- **Confirm with:** the company's release, its annual report or a tender notice.
- **Search:** `"<company>" vendor consolidation OR outsourcing 2026`, `<industry> bank transformation programme India partner`

### I3. A public tender in the seller's category
- **Why:** an open buying process with a deadline.
- **Strength:** strong. **Maximum age:** until the submission deadline.
- **Confirm with:** the notice on TED (Tenders Electronic Daily), the EU's public procurement journal.
- **Search:** TED search by CPV code and country, for example IT services (CPV 72000000) in Germany or the Netherlands.

### I4. The owner of the buying decision has just arrived
- **Roles:** head of vendor management, global sourcing, offshore delivery, India partnerships, strategic sourcing.
- **Strength:** medium. **Maximum age:** 4 months.
- **Confirm with:** the company's announcement or the person's public profile.
- **Search:** `appointed "head of global sourcing" OR "vendor management" <industry> <country>`

### I5. CBAM exposure
- **Applies to:** EU importers of CBAM goods (steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, hydrogen, electricity) that source from India.
- **Why:** they need embedded-emissions data from their Indian suppliers. That is a strong signal for compliance, data and verification offers, and a reason for Indian suppliers to buy help.
- **Strength:** strong for compliance or data offers, otherwise medium. **Maximum age:** ongoing. Check the current CBAM phase and thresholds on the European Commission's CBAM pages.
- **Search:** `CBAM Indian steel OR aluminium suppliers <EU country> importer`, German: `CBAM Lieferanten Indien Emissionsdaten`

### I6. A change in the EU-India Free Trade Agreement for the buyer's products
- **Why:** lower duties make Indian supply cheaper, and rules of origin need proof.
- **Strength:** medium. **Maximum age:** 12 months after the change takes effect.
- **Confirm with:** the European Commission's trade pages for the agreement's current status (negotiation, signature, entry into force) and the schedule for the product. Do not treat expected dates as facts.
- **Search:** `EU India FTA <product or HS chapter> tariff`, `EU-India trade agreement entry into force`

### I7. An Indian vendor won a deal at a peer of the account
- **Why:** the category is open to Indian vendors, and peers compare notes.
- **Strength:** weak. **Maximum age:** 6 months.
- **Confirm with:** the vendor's release or the stock-exchange disclosure of a listed Indian company.
- **Search:** `Indian IT company wins deal <European industry> <country> 2026`

### I8. A European company opens or grows a capability centre in India
- **Same event as E1, seen from the Indian side:** the centre buys local services such as staffing, facilities, IT services and training from Indian firms.
- **Strength:** medium. **Maximum age:** 9 months.

## Signals that are not enough on their own

- Attending a trade fair or a chamber event.
- A generic "India is a key market" line in an interview.
- An award or ranking.
- A funding round with no link to the corridor.

Use these to support a stronger signal, never as the reason for a lead.
